# 🌾 AgriNayan — Smart AI Precision Farming Co-Pilot

> **Empowering smallholder farmers with precision agriculture, regional voice advisories, bio-economy monetization, and direct marketplace access.**

---

## 📌 Overview

**AgriNayan** is an AI-driven, end-to-end farming co-pilot that replaces traditional agricultural guesswork with data-driven precision farming. By integrating hyper-local weather analytics, soil profiling, multi-crop land allocation, live mandi pricing, and biomass waste trading, AgriNayan guides farmers through every stage of the crop lifecycle—from seed selection to post-harvest sales.

Built with an inclusive **Voice-First & Multi-Lingual Engine**, AgriNayan bridges the digital and literacy divide by supporting **11 regional Indian languages** (including Hinglish) and custom named voice personas.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 1. 🗺️ Mitti & Zameen Profiler (Soil & Land Analytics)
* **Custom Field Profiling:** Collects Village, District, Soil Type (Loam, Clay, Sandy, Alluvial), Planting Season (Kharif, Rabi, Zaid), Land Area, and Farmer Budget.
* **Profit-Optimized Land Allocation:** Uses multi-variable algorithms to generate optimal crop division ratios (Primary, Secondary, and Nitrogen-Fixing Soil Restorer crops) to maximize financial yield and reduce synthetic fertilizer reliance.
* **Demo Farmer Loader:** Pre-fills realistic sample farm data for instant demonstration.

### 2. 📋 View Care Farm — Daily Inspection Checklist
* **Routine Crop Inspections:** Daily interactive checklists covering Field Cleanliness, Topsoil Moisture Tests, Weed Detection, Drip Pump & Irrigation Line Health, and Early Pest/Leaf Yellowing Detection.
* **Real-time Progress Tracker:** Keeps smallholder farmers on schedule to prevent avoidable crop yield loss.

### 3. 🎙️ Vernacular Voice Engine & Multi-Lingual UI
* **11 Supported Languages:** English, Hinglish, Hindi (हिन्दी), Kannada (ಕನ್ನಡ), Telugu (తెలుగు), Tamil (தமிழ்), Marathi (मराठी), Punjabi (ਪੰਜਾਬੀ), Gujarati (ગુજરાતી), Bengali (বাংলা), and Malayalam (മലയാളം).
* **Named Voice Personas:** Multi-voice engine selector featuring regional personas (*Ramesh Bhaiya*, *Priya Didi*, *Kisan Guru Gurpreet*).
* **Speech-to-Text & Text-to-Speech:** Web Speech API integration for real-time audio weather advisories and voice questions via *"AgriNayan Bol"*.

### 4. 🛒 AgriBazaar — Low-Cost Equipment & Subsidized Tools
* **Direct E-Commerce Integration:** Handpicked tools with direct buy links to **Amazon India**, **BigHaat**, and **Moglix**.
* **Essential Budget Hardware:** 4-in-1 Soil pH & Moisture Testers, 16L Battery Knapsack Sprayers, Mini Solar Insect Traps, and Micro Drip Irrigation Kits with highlighted government subsidy tags.

### 5. ♻️ Bio-Economy & Sustainability (Waste Monetization)
* **Stubble-to-Cash Blueprints:** Post-harvest waste processing models to eliminate crop residue burning.
* **Monetization Channels:** 
  * Paddy Stubble to Bio-Char / Thermal Pellets (₹1,800 – ₹2,500/Ton)
  * Oyster Mushroom Beds using Wheat Straw (₹120 – ₹180/Kg)
  * Vermicomposting Organic Fertilizer (₹8 – ₹12/Kg)

### 6. 🏛️ Government Schemes & Subsidy Eligibility Verifier
* **Multi-Point Verification:** Evaluates eligibility based on landholding size, Aadhaar-bank account linking status, category, annual income, soil card status, and solar pump requirement.
* **Direct Official Portal Access:** Direct external redirect links to official government portals:
  * [PM-KISAN Portal](https://pmkisan.gov.in/)
  * [PM-KUSUM Solar Pump Scheme](https://pmkusum.mnre.gov.in/)
  * [Soil Health Card Portal](https://soilhealth.dac.gov.in/)
  * [SMAM Mechanization](https://agrimachinery.nic.in/)
  * [PM Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)](https://pmfby.gov.in/)

### 7. 🎓 Interactive Tutorial & Safety Guide
* **Language-Specific Video Guides:** Regional language video options with direct YouTube tutorial embeds and links.
* **Step-by-Step Module Walkthroughs:** Comprehensive guides for Soil Profiling, Care Farm Inspections, Bio-Economy Monetization, and Tool Procurement.
* **Pesticide & Field Safety:** Clear protocols for PPE gear, safe chemical dilution, application timing, and storage rules.
