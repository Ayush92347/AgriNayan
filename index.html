Sustainability & Agricultural Bio-Economy

Turn farm waste (stubble/parali/husks) into additional revenue streams instead of burning it.

1. Stubble to Bio-Char & Pellets

Collect paddy straw using balers and sell directly to local bio-power plants or compress into fuel pellets.
